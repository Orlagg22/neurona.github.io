Biological vs. Artificial Neuron

A real neuron integrates thousands of noisy, timed chemical and electrical inputs across a living, rewiring structure. An artificial neuron reduces this to a weighted sum and a fixed activation function — useful for computation, but a huge simplification of what biology actually does.
